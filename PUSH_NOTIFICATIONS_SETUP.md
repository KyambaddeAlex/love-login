## 6. Use Love Login on iPhone and Android

Love Login is a progressive web app and can be installed from its secure website on both platforms. The Android APK remains available as a separate native option.

- **iPhone/iPad:** Open the site in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then launch Love Login from the new Home Screen icon. Web push notifications require iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later; sign in and enable alerts from the installed app.
- **Android:** Open the site in Chrome and choose **Install app** when prompted (or use Chrome's menu to install it). Sign in and enable alerts on each device.

Push permission is device-specific. The recipient must enable alerts on a device not also registered to the sender; shared devices are intentionally excluded to prevent self-notifications. Account data, messaging, and notification delivery require an internet connection.
